`take_text_proved` is in the new module `src/text_proved.bats`, beside `take_text` (unchanged). It takes an entry's data as a `barr` whose cells are in its type, plus the entry's method as `entry_method(kind)`, and gives the array back with a `text_taken(kind, cs)`. `TextTaken` carries `INFLATES(kind, cs, output)`, `TEXTOF(output, plan, TOUT_bytes(octets))` and `HOLDS(text, 0, octets, count)` over the same `output` cells. A deflated entry is inflated by zip's `inflate_cells_proved` (the in-memory form that returns `INFLATES`; `inflate_proved` reads and writes files and is native only, and quire is wasm). A stored entry is a copy of its data. The text is read by `text_plan_proved`, and converted by `text_convert_size` and `text_convert_fill` when it needs a conversion. Other outcomes have constructors of their own: refused (with its `TEXTOF`), empty (with its `INFLATES`), malformed, and too large.

Lemma `text_taken_functional`: the same compressed cells by the same method give the same plan and the same octets (`EQPLAN`, `EQB`), from `inflates_functional` and `textof_functional`.
