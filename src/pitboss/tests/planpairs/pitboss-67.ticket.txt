Owner rule (2026-10-26, from bats-lang/quire#905, whose qsweep is not changed; pitboss carries the feature): runner pressure must be absent for a quiet period before a hold lifts, because some jobs take a minute to trigger in a serial queue. And once a job is put on the queue it counts as immediate pressure, so stuffing the queue cannot eject entries (a dequeued entry restarts the merge queue and causes infinite holds).

Done when:
- `pitboss pressure` appends one line (time, `none` or `hold`) to a record file (config key `[runners] record`) at each call; it exits 0 only when the record shows no hold at any look for the last `[runners] quiet_minutes` minutes in a row (default 15), and exits 1 saying how many minutes remain otherwise; a hold line resets the quiet period.
- `pitboss queued` appends a hold line at once (called by the orchestrator after a merge, an enqueue or a push that went out); `pitboss push` appends one when its push goes out and nothing when it refuses, so a retried refused push does not keep resetting the wait.
- Tests with an injected clock and gh: a record whose newest hold is 14 minutes old gives exit 1, 16 minutes old gives exit 0; a push at minute 10 is refused and leaves the record unchanged (exit 0 at minute 15); a push that goes out at minute 16 gives exit 1 until minute 31; `pitboss queued` after a clear look resets the wait.
- The sweep skill's rules.md and ci.md state the rule once (the quiet period is the config key, not a number in the rule), and the missing-key note of ci.md for the quiet period is removed.
